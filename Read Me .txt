Potential Areas for Improvement / Interview Questions
To push this project to a 10/10, consider these common interview "deep dives":
Concurrency: How do you handle two people booking the same appointment slot at the exact same time?.
Error Handling: Do you have a global exception handler, and how do you log errors without exposing sensitive database info?.
Data Validation: Are you validating data only in the UI, or also in the Business Logic and Stored Procedures?.
Testing: Have you implemented Unit Tests for the Business Logic layer? This is often the final piece missing from junior portfolios
